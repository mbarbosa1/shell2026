The produce model is trained from in-store photos, not from the catalog's product images. See `Training/README.md`: photos taken with the intended iPhone in the store, one tight box per item drawn by a person, broad labels only (`onion`, `apple`, …), and train/validation/test split by physical specimen and capture session. It covers items whose item type is Fruit or Vegetables and that `visual-product-mappings.json` maps to a produce class; everything else is read with OCR. The model replaces Apple Vision only after it beats Apple Vision on the held-out test photos and meets the acceptance thresholds agreed before sign-off.

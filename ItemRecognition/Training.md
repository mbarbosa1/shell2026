Train the appearance model from each product's alternateImageURLs, which show the shape and color differences from that product's primary image URL in the same session. Use those pictures for items whose item type is Fruit or Vegetables, because those items are recognized with Apple Vision instead of OCR. Give every training picture one broad label, such as apple or onion, so variety and brand are not separate classes. Keep images that are not listed in alternateImageURLs out of training and accept the model only after it names those held-out pictures as the same product.
